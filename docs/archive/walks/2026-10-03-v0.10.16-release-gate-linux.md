## Findings

1. **The bundled GLib can't load the host's GIO modules.** Both the installed 0.10.15 and the updated 0.10.16 print on
   stderr `Failed to load module` for `libgvfsdbus.so` (`undefined symbol: g_task_set_static_name`),
   `libdconfsettings.so` (`g_assertion_message_cmpint`) and `libgioremote-volume-monitor.so`. Reasoned, not checked: the
   AppImage's bundled GLib is older than the host's. Behaviour was unaffected in this walk — *Open* and the HTTPS fetch
   both worked — but a feature that goes through GVFS (network locations, trash on a mount) or dconf wasn't tried. Also
   present: an `atk-bridge` warning and the usual Xvfb portal warnings.
2. **The desktop portal's GTK4 folder picker ignores a typed path.** The portal-served *Open repository* dialog
   did not accept the path `xdialog.sh` types into it; a harness note, not a product finding — see
   `docs/smoke/smoke-linux.md`.
