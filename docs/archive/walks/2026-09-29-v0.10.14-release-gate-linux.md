## Findings

1. **The 0.10.13 → 0.10.14 update installs in place and needs a manual start, as the release notes say.**
   - The failed restart is the old app's: 0.10.13 spawns the new file under its own `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, and `AppRun`'s
     `/usr/bin/env` fails on the bundled `libsystemd` (the 0.10.13 gate's finding 1).
   - The journal shows two images unmounting at the update: `tmp-.mount_T4\x2dGitIABleP` at 00:26:47.824, then the
     old app's `tmp-.mount_T4\x2dGitKgoOIf` at .854. Reading, not verified: the first is the new file, mounted by
     the failed restart and dropped when its `AppRun` failed.
   - The first update that restarts by itself will be 0.10.14 → the next release.
2. **One apport entry during the gate, from 0.10.13, not 0.10.14.** At 00:25:26.800:
   `executable was modified after program start, ignoring`, with no `called for` line. It is the same signature as
   the unexplained entry after group BI's row 1–7 session (open-items §Q).
   - It came 111 ms before the stopped blank 0.10.13 instance's image unmounted (00:25:26.911). That instance was
     stopped with SIGTERM several seconds earlier (not timed), and its image stayed mounted until then.
   - Reading, not verified: a WebKit helper left behind from that image crashed on its way out. apport can't see
     inside the user's FUSE mount, so it records neither the process nor the signal.
   - A data point for the §Q row: the same signature also comes from 0.10.13, a build without the hotfix's fd
     change, after its app was killed. So the signature isn't unique to the hotfix build.
3. **As shipped, 0.10.13 still opens blank on this VMware desktop.** `WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=1` alone
   fixes it, as in the README note, as on the 0.10.13 gate. 0.10.14 as shipped wasn't tried on the desktop, only on
   Xvfb, where it renders.
