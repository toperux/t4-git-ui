## Findings

- **G1 — a new stderr line, "A connection to the bus can't be made".** After the update, stderr no longer shows the
  0.10.18-era `libgvfscommon`/`libgvfsdbus` pairs (those were 0.10.18's, logged before its `atk-bridge` line); only
  "A connection to the bus can't be made" (new; source not identified, likely `atk-bridge` under the private bus)
  and the usual `libEGL` DRI3 pair. Caveat: this assumes the relaunched process kept the same stderr (fd 2 wasn't
  checked directly). **Accepted, closed** by the owner as harness noise.
- **G2 — the editor opened on Xvfb instead of the real desktop.** Unlike the v0.10.18 gate, where *Open* handed off
  to the live GNOME session, this run's GNOME Text Editor started on the harness's own `:98` over a private bus.
  **Accepted, closed** by the owner: a harness difference between the two gate runs, cause not traced.
- **G3 — the first XTEST click on a menu item was swallowed.** Opening `.editorconfig` from the Files list needed
  two clicks: the first only highlighted the item, the second activated it. Not reproduced elsewhere in this walk;
  not known whether it's the harness or the app. **Accepted, with a reopen trigger** — recorded in `open-items.md`
  §Q.
- **G4 — the display had to be sized to the store's saved window.** T26's stall (a saved window taller than the
  display) forced 1920 × 1200 in Setup above, rather than the usual 1600 × 1000. Recorded as a clause in
  `docs/smoke/smoke-linux.md`'s *Give it a display of its own* bullet.

New for 0.10.19 otherwise:
- N8 in its first session after the update, as accepted (`open-items.md` §Q): the restarted app was started with
  0.10.18's `GDK_BACKEND=x11`, so `main.rs` recorded `T4_HOST_GDK_BACKEND=x11`. The WebKit helpers show
  `GDK_BACKEND=x11`, `T4_HOST_GDK_BACKEND=x11` and `GSETTINGS_BACKEND=memory`, and every program the app started
  (the fetch's git, `xdg-open`, `gio`, the editor) got `GDK_BACKEND=x11`. None got `GSETTINGS_BACKEND` or a
  `T4_HOST_*` name.
- No `libgvfsdbus.so` "Failed to load module" warning from 0.10.19 (caveat above).
