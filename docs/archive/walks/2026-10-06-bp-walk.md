### BP 4 on Linux, in detail

`/tmp/t4/g3many` built per the recipe (600 `file-NNN.txt` files, one commit), Xvfb at 1920×1200, openbox maximized
(lands at 1920×1179, y 21); launched by hand under `dbus-run-session`; dark theme.

Four runs on `file-002`/`004`/`006`/`008`: the row's native tooltip (96×47, at x 645) sits over the context menu,
next to *Open*, at 1 s, in 4 of 4; a no-pause click arriving on *Open*'s icon lands on the tooltip instead and is
lost in 4 of 4 (*Open* stays highlighted, the menu stays open — the gate's own failure picture); a control click
beside the tooltip opened the file in 1 of 1.

A DOM read under `tauri-driver` confirmed the row's `title` attribute IS stripped while the menu is open (row 4's
fix is in effect). Hovering 0.3 s then right-clicking showed no tooltip window, in 2 of 2 observations. A
one-motion move-and-right-click (no hover pause first) showed the tooltip window anyway, with the OLD text, in 2
of 2. Inferred, not traced: WebKitGTK reads the tooltip text off the pointer's motion event before the
`contextmenu` handler strips the attribute, and GTK's already-scheduled tooltip isn't cancelled by the later
attribute change.

Owner's ruling (D35): keep row 4 regardless — it is a real improvement (the Mac passes, and the DOM confirms the
strip runs), even though it doesn't close this one Linux path. T15 and G3 (`open-items.md` §Z and §Q) stay open
with this measurement; the gate's G3 check is now a record of a known app/WebKitGTK interaction, not a release
blocker.

## BP 7 — Windows, fail on `5392166`, fix, re-walk

**Fail, intermittent, on `5392166`:** across roughly 30 switch sequences between two tabs (`work`, holding a
selection on `f27769f`, a pair of near-identical commits; `bp`), `work`'s selection came back on HEAD (`d46f5c3`)
instead of `f27769f` in 5 of them, all between 21:14 and 21:21 local time, including at least one single switch
(not only the rapid ×20 kind). One "slow status … 781 ms" log line fell inside that window. The `bp` tab never
lost its selection.

**Cause**, traced from the 5 losses: the reselect/reanchor logic judged a stale `null` lookup — asked of a
half-walked backend while the restarted walk was still running — against the log's post-await `complete` flag,
so a result that arrived late enough looked like "the commit really isn't there any more" instead of "not loaded
yet".

**Fix:** `1aa5565` — reselect and reanchor now read `log.complete` from before the `await`, not after, so a stale
`null` can no longer outrun the state it was read against (D37). `1aa5565` carries its own reselect test;
`b3f6d79` adds reanchor's, `34dbdae` a comment fix.

**Re-walk on `34dbdae`, pass, 21 of 21 kept:** 11 of the 20-switch scripted repro, 10 single switches; 13 of the 21
run under added CPU load (L1: 8 busy runspaces, CPU 84–85%; L2: +24 more runspaces, CPU 97% down to 70%). 15 "slow
status" lines, 254–295 ms, appeared under L2; 268 reselect walks total, the slowest 94 ms; no WARN or ERROR in the
log. Caveat: the losing run's slow status was 781 ms; this re-walk's load only reached about 300 ms, so the race
window it exercised may not have been as wide as the one that lost (inferred, not measured directly).
