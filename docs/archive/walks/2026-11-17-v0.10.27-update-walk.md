# In-app update walk — 0.10.26 → published 0.10.27, 2026-11-17

The update path walked on the release after `docs/archive/walks/2026-11-14-v0.10.26-update-walk.md`. On the Windows VM
the installed copy was walked through the rows *Offered on launch*, *The install*, *The restart*, *Up to date*,
*Outside the app* and *Log and console*. On the Linux VM the published 0.10.26 AppImage was walked through five rows:
1 Offered, 2 The install, 3 The restart, 4 Up to date, 5 Quit and log. Both VMs were driven over Remote Control and
reported back; nothing was ticked or committed there. Every reading below was measured on the VM unless it says
otherwise.

It is the walk that the open row in `docs/plans/open-items.md` §AW waited for. 0.10.26 is the first release whose
installed copy carries three things, and all three ran for the first time from an installed copy in this update:

- `tauri-plugin-updater` 2.14.1, whose signature check runs on `base64` 0.23.1;
- the AppImage relaunch that releases the single-instance lock before it starts the new copy (`2f6740bb`, in #42's
  merge `e147d30a`);
- the Windows update path's release of the lock just before the installer starts.

Both briefs added a process watch around the restart and a second start of the app by hand. The Linux VM added, by its
own choice, a second full run on a session bus that outlives the old process.
