## What counts as a difference

Counted: layout and order; spacing and sizes off by ≥ 2 px; a colour that is a different token (checked with
`getComputedStyle` over `cdp.mjs --eval` when the eye can't tell); a missing, extra or renamed control or label; a
different icon; a state the canvas draws that the app can't reach.

Not counted: the data (the canvases draw a made-up t4-git-ui history; the fixtures differ); font antialiasing; text
widths from a different string; what R1, D2–D4 and A–C leave out.

Light is compared in full. **Dark is compared for colour only**: the dark token block holds only colours and shadows
(`build/tokens.css:133-204`), and `src/**/*.css` has no other `data-theme` selector (checked in review passes 1 and 2),
so a layout difference found in light counts for both.
